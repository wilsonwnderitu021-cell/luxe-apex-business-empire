Refund Policy

Luxury You Deserve. Service You Trust.

At Luxe & Apex Business Empire, your satisfaction is our priority. If something isn't right with your order, we're here to make it right.

Eligibility for a Refund

  • Request a refund within 7 days of receiving your item.
  • Item must be unused, in original condition and packaging with tags.
  • Proof of purchase (order number or M-Pesa confirmation) required.
  • Faulty or damaged items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery.

Non-Refundable Items

  • Cars — all vehicle sales are final after inspection.
  • Items damaged through misuse or returned without original packaging.
  • Hygiene-sensitive goods once opened, unless faulty.

How Refunds Are Processed

  • Issued via M-Pesa or original payment method.
  • Processed within 3–5 business days of approval.
  • Store credit available as an alternative.
  • Delivery fees non-refundable unless our error or faulty item.

How to Request

WhatsApp us on 0793 397 813 with your order number and reason.



Exchange Policy

Luxury You Deserve. Service You Trust.

Prefer a different size, colour, or item? We're happy to arrange an exchange.

Eligibility for an Exchange

  • Request within 7 days of receiving your item.
  • Item must be unused, in original condition and packaging with tags.
  • Proof of purchase required.
  • Faulty items reported within 48 hours get a priority exchange.

Items That Cannot Be Exchanged

  • Cars — all vehicle sales are final after inspection.
  • Items damaged through misuse or without original packaging.
  • Hygiene-sensitive goods once opened, unless faulty.

How Exchanges Work

  • Replacement dispatched once your item is received and approved.
  • Price differences settled via M-Pesa or store credit.
  • Delivery fees may apply except for our error or faulty items.
  • Out-of-stock items: alternative, refund, or store credit offered.

How to Request

WhatsApp us on 0793 397 813 with your order number and the item you'd like instead.
